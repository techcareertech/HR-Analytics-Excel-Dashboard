# Strategic HR Analytics: Reducing Attrition & Improving Pay Equity

## 📌 Executive Summary
This project analyzes 1,000 employee records for a multinational corporation to address a rising **8.5% annual turnover rate**. By synthesizing demographic, compensation, and tenure data, I identified critical attrition "hotspots" and uncovered a significant **$8,600 ethnic pay gap**. 

The final interactive dashboard provides HR leadership with a data-driven roadmap to improve retention, potentially saving the organization **$500K - $1M annually** in recruitment and productivity costs.

---

## 🚀 The Business Problem
The organization was losing high-value talent without a clear understanding of the "Why." Leadership lacked visibility into whether exits were driven by compensation, tenure burnout, or cultural issues. This project answers:
1. **Retention:** Why is Marketing turnover 48% higher than the company average?
2. **Equity:** Is there a statistically significant pay gap across demographic groups?
3. **Risk:** Which geographic locations and tenure brackets are currently "at-risk"?

---

## 📊 Key Insights & Root Cause Analysis

### 1. The Marketing "Onboarding Crisis"
* **Finding:** Marketing has the highest turnover at **12.6%**.
* **Root Cause:** 60% of these exits occur within the **first year**. 
* **Insight:** Data suggests a misalignment in the hiring/onboarding process rather than long-term burnout.

### 2. The Engineering "Senior Talent Drain"
* **Finding:** Engineering turnover is **10.8%**.
* **Root Cause:** 70% of Engineering exits happen **after 3+ years**. 
* **Insight:** The company is losing its most experienced technical talent at the 3-year mark.

### 3. Diversity & Pay Equity
* **Finding:** Black employee turnover is **13.5%** (59% higher than average).
* **Root Cause:** Analysis revealed an **$8,600 annual pay gap** for Black employees compared to the company mean.

---

## 🛠 Technical Implementation (Excel & Power Query)
I followed a professional ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) workflow to ensure data integrity:

* **Power Query:** Automated cleaning of 1,000+ records, handled nulls in `Exit Date`, and created conditional logic for "Employment Status."
* **Data Modeling:** Built a robust structure using Pivot Tables to aggregate data across Geography, Ethnicity, and Tenure.
* **Calculated Measures:** Developed formulas for *Attrition Rate %*, *Variance from Mean Salary*, and *Tenure Cohort Bucketing*.
* **Interactive Design:** Implemented dynamic **Slicers** to allow executives to drill down into specific regions like Beijing (23.5% turnover).

---

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations

1. **Pay Equity Audit:** Close the $8,600 gap for Black employees to mitigate legal and retention risks.
2. **3-Year Retention Bonus:** Launch a tiered bonus program for Engineering roles to prevent senior talent drain.
3. **Marketing "Buddy System":** Overhaul the first 90 days of onboarding to address high Year-1 turnover.
4. **Stay Interviews:** Conduct interviews at the 24-month mark to identify flight risks early.
